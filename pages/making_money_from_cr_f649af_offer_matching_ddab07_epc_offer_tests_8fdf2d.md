## Introduction

In affiliate websites, the *headline* commission rate — 30% here, 5% there — rarely tells the full story of which offer actually makes money from a page’s traffic. What really matters to [revenue per visitor]({{ 'revenue-model/' | relative_url }}) is **Earnings Per Click (EPC)** — a normalised measure of how much each click on an affiliate link earns on average. Unlike commission rate alone, EPC combines conversion rate, average order value, and payout into one simple number that shows what your traffic *actually produced*. That makes EPC the practical performance metric for choosing and testing affiliate offers in a scalable, evidence‑driven website system.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://track360.io/glossary/epc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: track360.io">[Track360]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">track360.io</span><span class="citation-popover-title">What is EPC (Earnings Per Click)? &#124; Affiliate Marketing Glossary &#124; Track360</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What is EPC (Earnings Per Click)? &#124; Affiliate Marketing Glossary &#124; Track360...</span></span></span>

## Why Commission Rates Mislead and EPC Matters More

Many operators assume the offer with the highest stated commission rate will earn the most. That’s a vanity metric — promising on paper but unconnected to how visitors behave after they click. EPC, by contrast, is calculated as revenue from affiliate links **divided by clicks** over a set period, giving a reliable view of the *value of traffic* to an offer. For example, an offer paying £40 at a 2% conversion rate yields an EPC of £0.80; another paying £20 at a 5% conversion rate yields £1.00 — *despite the lower payout*. EPC collapses conversion and payout into one number you can compare directly.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.hostinger.com/uk/tutorials/what-is-epc-in-affiliate-marketing" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hostinger.com">[Hostinger]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hostinger.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Earnings Per Click: What is EPC in affiliate marketing</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Earnings Per Click: What is EPC in affiliate marketingApril 23, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 23, 2025</span></span></span>

In a scaled website context this matters because pages will vary greatly in user intent: a “how to fix X” page versus a “best deals on X” page might send very different quality traffic to the same affiliate, so EPC reveals where one offer earns more per click than another. Using the commission percentage alone would miss these patterns entirely.[partnero.com]

## How EPC Captures Real Click Value

### EPC as the Practical Performance Signal

At its core, EPC shows *what happened* — not what the programme promised. The basic formula everyone uses in affiliate reporting tools and networks is:

> EPC = Total affiliate earnings ÷ total tracked clicks

Networks display it over set windows (7-day, 30-day, etc.) to smooth noise, but the core idea is the same: a higher EPC means your clicks are converting more profitably.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://track360.io/glossary/epc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: track360.io">[Track360]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">track360.io</span><span class="citation-popover-title">What is EPC (Earnings Per Click)? &#124; Affiliate Marketing Glossary &#124; Track360</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What is EPC (Earnings Per Click)? &#124; Affiliate Marketing Glossary &#124; Track360...</span></span></span>

This number integrates three key drivers:

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--metric" markdown="1">

* **Conversion rate** — how many of your clicks turn into qualifying actions (purchases, leads, etc.).
* **Average order value** — larger baskets = more commission per conversion.
* **Commission payout rules** — fixed or percentage structures.

</div>

By design, EPC gets past the trap of evaluating offers in isolation. A low‑commission offer can outperform a high‑commission one if it converts better or has a larger average sale value — and EPC will show that in plain numbers.[partnero.com]

### EPC Across Networks and Contexts

## Testing Offers with EPC by Page Type

Smart affiliate systems don’t pick one offer and plaster it everywhere. Instead, they **test multiple offers and compare EPC results by page intent and traffic type**.

1. **Set Up Parallel Offer Tests:**

On pages with similar intent (e.g. product [comparisons]({{ 'comparisons/' | relative_url }})) deploy two or more offers that look relevant semantically and contextually to the visitor. Track clicks and subsequent earnings separately. Over a sample period (say 30–90 days), calculate the EPC for each offer variant.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://trackier.com/epc-tracking-why-it-matters-for-affiliate-earnings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: trackier.com">[Trackier]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">trackier.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">EPC Tracking: Why It Matters for Affiliate Boost Earnings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EPC Tracking: Why It Matters for Affiliate Boost EarningsDecember 11, 2025 — EPC tracking (Earnings Per Click tracking) measures...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 11, 2025</span></span></span>
2. **Compare EPC by Segment:**

Break EPC down by page type (tutorial, review, comparison) and traffic source. A page with highly transactional intent might reveal a different best‑performing offer than an informational page. This segmentation shows which offer best matches *that specific intent*.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://trackier.com/epc-tracking-why-it-matters-for-affiliate-earnings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: trackier.com">[Trackier]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">trackier.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">EPC Tracking: Why It Matters for Affiliate Boost Earnings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EPC Tracking: Why It Matters for Affiliate Boost EarningsDecember 11, 2025 — EPC tracking (Earnings Per Click tracking) measures...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 11, 2025</span></span></span>
3. **Iterate and Replace:**

If an alternative offer shows consistently higher EPC on a specific page type, promote it more heavily or remove the lower‑performing offer. Over time, this standardises offer choice by empirical value from your own traffic, not generic network averages.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://trackier.com/epc-tracking-why-it-matters-for-affiliate-earnings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: trackier.com">[Trackier]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">trackier.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">EPC Tracking: Why It Matters for Affiliate Boost Earnings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EPC Tracking: Why It Matters for Affiliate Boost EarningsDecember 11, 2025 — EPC tracking (Earnings Per Click tracking) measures...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 11, 2025</span></span></span>
4. **Use Real‑World Benchmarks Carefully:**

Many networks publish “network EPC” numbers as benchmarks, but those are aggregate across all publishers. Your audience may behave differently; use network EPC only as a rough orientation, then prioritise your *own* EPC tests.[partnero.com]

## Practical Metrics and Decision Rules

EPC tests are most useful when integrated with simple decision rules a scalable system can follow:

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--decision" markdown="1">

* **Always prefer offers with higher EPC for the same traffic segment** — even if their nominal payout is lower.
* **Require a minimum sample size (e.g. >200–500 clicks)** before deciding to switch offers to avoid premature conclusions from small samples.
* **Monitor trends over time** — a drop in EPC may signal changes in conversion behaviour or traffic quality, prompting reevaluation.
* **Segment by device and geo** — because EPC can vary significantly across audiences.[partnero.com]

</div>

These tests turn offer selection from a static choice based on headline commission into an ongoing optimisation process rooted in evidence that *predicts real revenue per visitor*. EPC itself doesn’t guarantee profitability, but it tells you which offers are genuinely making money from the clicks you already have.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://track360.io/glossary/epc" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: track360.io">[Track360]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">track360.io</span><span class="citation-popover-title">What is EPC (Earnings Per Click)? &#124; Affiliate Marketing Glossary &#124; Track360</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">What is EPC (Earnings Per Click)? &#124; Affiliate Marketing Glossary &#124; Track360...</span></span></span>

## Summary

EPC tests are the practical mechanism affiliate websites use to answer the question “Which offer actually earns more from my specific page and traffic?” Instead of relying on commission rates alone, EPC combines payout, conversion behaviour, and average order economics into one easily comparable number. By running structured offer tests and comparing EPC by page intent and traffic segment, scalable affiliate systems can standardise offer selection, improve real revenue per click, and build repeatable patterns that inform templates, internal linking, and content choices.
